# OUDIE CRO Skill

- `/docs/07_CRO_COMMERCE.md`

## CRO hierarchy

Do not optimize conversion by making the site louder. Reduce uncertainty instead.

For fragrance shoppers, the main uncertainties are:

- what it smells like;
- whether they will like it;
- format differences;
- price;
- trust;
- delivery/returns;
- what to sample first.

## Product cards

Always expose:

- identity;
- scent descriptor/notes;
- price or `From $X`;
- relevant formats;
- clear click target.

Do not hide essential commerce information until hover.

## Sampling

Treat samples as a confidence mechanism. Strong placements:

- homepage discovery section;
- PDP “Try it first” CTA;
- Scent Explorer result;
- cart upsell when relevant.

Do not invent a sample-credit policy.

## PDP

Above the fold must make buying possible without hunting.

Use reviews near the decision. Preserve Judge.me rather than duplicating review UI from another data source.

## Cart

Free shipping threshold: CAD $120.

A prior AOV snapshot was around CAD $106.97, so a tasteful shipping progress cue can be helpful. Keep upsell relevance high and quantity low.

## Experiences

Lead conversion should come from clarity:

- what it is;
- what is included;
- who it is for;
- proof;
- inquiry CTA.

Never auto-quote pricing unless explicitly approved by the merchant.

## Anti-patterns

Avoid:

- fake urgency;
- countdown timers without real deadline logic;
- excessive badges;
- sticky elements covering content;
- five CTAs competing in one viewport;
- aggressive popups before users understand the brand;
- unrelated cart cross-sells.
